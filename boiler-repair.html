Boiler Repair

If your boiler is not heating properly, making unusual noises or showing signs of a fault, Eagle Heating & Plumbing can help with practical boiler repair support and quick response for urgent issues.

Common boiler issues

  • No heating or no hot water.
  • Pressure problems.
  • Strange noises or repeated faults.
  • Radiators not heating properly.
  • Leaks around the boiler or pipework.

Always make sure any boiler work is handled safely and in line with the correct qualifications and regulations.
